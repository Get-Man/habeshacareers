SMART CITY BACKGROUND IMAGE INSTRUCTIONS
=========================================

To add the Addis Ababa city background image:

1. Find or download a high-quality image of Addis Ababa city skyline
   - Recommended size: 1920x1080 or larger
   - Format: JPG or PNG
   - Look for images showing modern buildings, cityscape, or aerial view

2. Save the image as: addis-ababa-city.jpg
   
3. Place it in the images folder (same folder as this file)

4. The image will automatically appear as the background of the "Our Trusted Partners" section

ALTERNATIVE:
If you want to use a different filename, update line 2085 in css/style.css:
Change: background: url('../images/addis-ababa-city.jpg')
To: background: url('../images/YOUR-IMAGE-NAME.jpg')

The image will have a white semi-transparent overlay (85% opacity) so the partner logos remain readable.

You can adjust the overlay opacity in css/style.css by changing:
background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85);
                              ^^^^
                              Change this value (0.0 to 1.0)
                              0.85 = 85% white overlay
                              0.5 = 50% white overlay
                              0.0 = no overlay (full image visibility)
