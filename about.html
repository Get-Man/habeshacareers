About Habesha Careers

Our Mission

Habesha Careers is dedicated to connecting talented professionals with meaningful career opportunities across Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa. We strive to bridge the gap between job seekers and employers, fostering economic growth and professional development in our region.

Our Vision

To become the leading job platform in Ethiopia, empowering individuals to achieve their career aspirations while helping organizations find the right talent to drive their success.

Our Values

  • Accessibility: Making job opportunities available to everyone
  • Integrity: Maintaining transparency and trust in all our interactions
  • Excellence: Delivering quality service to both job seekers and employers
  • Community: Supporting the growth and development of our local workforce

Who We Serve

Job Seekers

Fresh graduates, experienced professionals, and career changers looking for opportunities

Employers

Local businesses, international NGOs, and organizations seeking qualified talent

Students

University students and recent graduates exploring career paths and internships

Our Platform

Habesha Careers provides a user-friendly platform where employers can post job openings and job seekers can discover opportunities that match their skills and aspirations. We focus on various sectors including IT, healthcare, NGO development, engineering, education, and finance.
